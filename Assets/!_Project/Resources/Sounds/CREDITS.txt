Each <kind>.ogg here is one of those files trimmed, faded and levelled (some pitched: the three stage-clear stars).
The game plays a file by its name (DefenseSounds, UISound), so renaming one changes which sound plays where.
alarm.ogg, hover.ogg: made in this project, not from a pack (Editor/UI~/make_sounds.py): the alarm is one short low 98 Hz buzzing
pulse ("징", .45 s), the hover a soft 160 ms G4 marimba tap (picked from HoverSounds/ candidates).
